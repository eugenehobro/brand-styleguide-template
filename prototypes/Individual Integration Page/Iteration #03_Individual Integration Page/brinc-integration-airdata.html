About this integration

AirData is a flight-data management platform used by drone programs to centralize logs, maintenance records, and compliance documentation across mixed fleets of aircraft.

This integration stores Guardian's flight logs in AirData alongside logs from your other drone platforms, streamlining reporting and maintenance tracking for programs that fly more than one type of aircraft.

Useful for FAA compliance reporting, fleet utilization analysis, and giving program managers a single source of truth for flight history regardless of which drone flew the mission.

Available now; connect your AirData account from your BRINC LiveOps settings.

Benefits

  • Single flight-history record across every aircraft in your fleet, not just Guardian
  • Faster FAA compliance reporting, without manual log exports between systems
  • Fleet-wide utilization data that supports smarter maintenance scheduling
  • One source of truth for program managers, regardless of which drone flew the mission
