ShotSpotter

Instant gunshot alerts, routed straight to your Guardian fleet.

RTC Law Enforcement
Video
Image
Cover image — final artwork TBD

About this integration

ShotSpotter is an acoustic gunshot detection network deployed across hundreds of cities, using rooftop- and streetlight-mounted sensors to detect, locate, and classify gunfire within seconds.

Paired with BRINC LiveOps, every confirmed ShotSpotter alert — including its precise location and confirmation status — is sent directly to your Guardian dispatch queue, so a drone can be in the air before a 911 call for the same incident even comes in.

Use cases include faster first-on-scene awareness for shots-fired calls, reduced risk to responding officers by seeing the scene before arrival, and a documented, time-stamped record connecting acoustic detection to aerial response for after-action review.

Available today for all Guardian customers with an active ShotSpotter subscription — no additional hardware required. Setup is handled through your BRINC LiveOps account team.

Benefits

  • Faster first-on-scene awareness for shots-fired calls, before officers arrive
  • Reduced risk to responding officers by seeing the scene from the air first
  • A time-stamped record linking acoustic detection to aerial response
  • No additional hardware required for agencies with an active subscription

Turn every 911 call into an instant drone launch point.

CAD Law Enforcement Fire EMS
Video
Image
Cover image — final artwork TBD

About this integration

Motorola's NG911 call-handling platform is the backbone of modern emergency communications centers, managing call intake, location data, and call routing for 911 centers nationwide.

The LiveOps integration passes calls-for-service information — including the caller's location — from NG911 directly into Guardian, letting dispatchers launch a drone to a verified location without re-keying a single address.

This removes the manual hand-off between call-taking and drone dispatch, reducing time-to-launch and eliminating a common source of dispatch error: wrong or incomplete addresses.

Available now. Setup is coordinated between your NG911 system administrator and the BRINC LiveOps onboarding team.

Benefits

  • Drone dispatch to a verified location without re-keying a single address
  • Removes the manual hand-off between call-taking and drone dispatch
  • Reduces time-to-launch on active calls
  • Eliminates a common source of dispatch error: wrong or incomplete addresses

Live911

Hear the call as it happens, right alongside your drone feed.

CAD Law Enforcement Fire EMS
Video
Image
Cover image — final artwork TBD

About this integration

Live911 streams live 911 call audio and location data directly to first responders in the field — giving officers and drone pilots the same real-time picture dispatch has.

With this integration, Live911's call location and streaming audio feed into BRINC LiveOps alongside your Guardian video feed, so the RTCC or responding pilot can listen to the call unfold while watching the scene from the air.

This is especially valuable for ambiguous or evolving calls — domestic disturbances, active alarms, welfare checks — where hearing tone and context from the caller changes how a scene should be approached.

Available now for agencies running Live911 alongside Guardian.

Benefits

  • Pilots and RTCC staff hear the call while watching the scene from the air
  • Especially valuable for ambiguous or evolving calls
  • Call location and streaming audio arrive alongside your Guardian video feed
  • No separate system needed to follow the call in progress

Every flight's footage, filed automatically.

About this integration

"Evidence" is BRINC's digital evidence management pairing — automatically routing footage and telemetry from every Guardian flight into your agency's evidence retention workflow.

As soon as a flight ends, video, snapshots, and flight metadata are packaged and sent to your connected evidence account, with chain-of-custody metadata intact — no manual export or file transfer required.

  • Closes the gap of evidence sitting on a laptop or SD card

Video
Image

About this integration

Vigilant Solutions' automated license plate reader (ALPR) network flags vehicles of interest — stolen vehicles, wanted persons, Amber Alerts — in real time as they pass fixed or mobile readers.

This integration sends Vigilant's license plate hit locations directly to BRINC LiveOps, giving agencies the option to launch a Guardian drone to visually confirm and track a flagged vehicle before committing patrol units.

  • Reduces risk in pursuit-adjacent scenarios with an early eye in the air
  • Hit locations reach BRINC LiveOps in real time
  • No manual step between a plate hit and a drone launch
